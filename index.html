About

I am a Master's student in Control Science and Engineering at Northeastern University, Shenyang, China. My research centers on multimodal fusion for 3D object detection, aiming to build robust perception systems that jointly exploit complementary sensor modalities such as cameras and LiDAR.

Beyond research, I work on the engineering side of these systems — efficient deep learning pipelines, C++ backend development, and embedded platforms — to bring perception models closer to real-world deployment.

Research Interests
